About

Hello, my name is Dielson and I’m a software developer from Maceió, Brazil.

Skills

I’ve been working in the industry for more about 10 years and graduated as a Bachelor of Information Systems at Instituto Federal de Alagoas (IFAL).

I have hands-on experience working with quite a few programming languages over the years, which include:

  • Objective-C
  • Swift
  • Java/Kotlin
  • C++ with Qt
  • Python (with a focus on NLP)
  • Typescript (mostly because of Parse Server back in the day)

I dedicated a year to working with Natural Language Processing (NLP) at Weni (former Ilhasoft), driven by my interest in machine learning. In fact, this experience motivated me to choose it as the focus of my graduation thesis.

Currently working as an iOS software engineer at Zup Innovation, mostly in the banking sector and serving millions of users, where I’ve focused mostly in Swift and a brief time in React.

Interests

I started my programming journey with my fascination for smartphones, which led me to get into programming for Blackberry 10, Android, and eventually iOS. Throughout my whole career, I have primarily focused on mobile development for smartphones, only recently expanding my hyper-focus to other areas of software development such as web development.

I also take pleasure in expanding my knowledge by reading about many programming languages and frameworks, even when I have no intention of using them, as I think it’s important and healthy to understand their advantages and drawbacks. This perpetual quest for knowledge fuels my enthusiasm for programming in general.

Ever since I spent some time working with NLP, I’ve been really interested in playing with LLMs and exploring their strengths and limitations. Recently (as of Oct 2026) I’ve got a new Mac Mini with 32GB of RAM just so I can run and experiment with more models locally.
